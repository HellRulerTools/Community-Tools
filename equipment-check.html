Fair question — here's exactly what it does, and what it doesn't.

  • Reads the character data already loaded on the game page (your gear, bag, tree, skills, level, depth).
  • Copies a trimmed snapshot to your clipboard. You paste it here, and everything runs in your own browser.
  • No uploading. It makes no network requests — nothing is sent to us or anyone. The data never leaves your browser.
  • No login details. It never reads your cookies, password, session or account token — only the game state.
  • No changes to your game. It only reads. It can't edit your character, spend anything, or act for you.
  • Nothing stored. It runs once when you click it and keeps nothing.

The only thing the snapshot contains is your character and bag — so don't paste it anywhere public. On this page it stays in memory and is gone when you close the tab.
